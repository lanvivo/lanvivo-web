Platform features

Everything the immigrant buyer actually needs.

Zillow is built for the average American buyer. Lanvivo is built for the one with an ITIN, a work visa, an OPT card, and a plan.

Market intelligence
Zipcode Rater
Score any US rental market across 8 investor dimensions (vacancy, crime, income, jobs, schools, cost of living, renter demand, and landlord law) using Census ACS, FBI UCR, and HUD data. Enter a zip code, get an A–D grade with a plain-English verdict instantly.
Market intelligence
Zipcode ROI
Enter a zip code and purchase price. Get gross yield, cap rate, cash-on-cash return, monthly cash flow, DSCR, and up to a 30-year appreciation projection, all pulled from HUD Fair Market Rents and Census data. No property address needed.
Mortgage
Visa-Aware Mortgage Engine
Enter your visa type, income, credit score, and down payment. The engine tells you which loan products (DSCR, ITIN, conventional, FHA) actually work for your situation, with plain-English next steps.
Analysis
Property Cash Flow Analyzer
Enter price, rent, taxes, and expenses. See monthly cash flow, cap rate, cash-on-cash return, DSCR, after-tax returns with depreciation, and a 15-year equity projection. Save deals and compare up to four side by side.
Comps & pricing
Property Analysis Report
Enter any US address. Pull recent comparable sales, rental rates, and how long homes actually take to sell in that market, then get a branded PDF with a competitive price range and specific recommendations. Built for sellers deciding what to list at, and buyers deciding what to offer.
Reports
Shareable Property Reports
Generate a clean PDF from any saved analysis and share it with a spouse, CPA, lender, or family member abroad. Or send a read-only link anyone can open without signing up. No app download required.
One-on-one
Talk to a Specialist
When the numbers aren't the problem and you just need a person: book a 45-minute session with someone who works with ITIN, visa-holder, and foreign-national buyers every week. $120, recorded, yours to keep.
